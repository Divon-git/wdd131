Project Planner Coming Soon

A future Tile Settings feature

Bring Your Room Plans Together

The Project Planner is an idea for a future version of Tile Settings. Instead of planning one room at a time, you could organize several rooms under a single flooring project.

This feature is beyond the scope of the current assignment, but it represents one way Tile Settings could grow into a more useful flooring-planning tool.

Start with Room Measurements

How it could work

From an Empty Room to a Project Plan

Here is a preview of the steps a future project planner could help visitors organize.

01

Name Your Project

Give your project a name, such as Home Renovation, New Apartment, or Living Room Upgrade.

02

Add Your Rooms

Create a list of rooms and record each room's length, width, and floor area.

03

Choose Tile Sizes

Select a tile size for each room, such as 40 × 40 cm, 60 × 60 cm, or 60 × 120 cm.

04

Review Your Plan

View a summary of your rooms and estimated tile quantities to help prepare for purchasing.
