Four simple steps

How to Measure a Room

You only need a tape measure, a place to record your numbers, and a little care.

01

Gather Your Tools

Get a tape measure, a pen, and paper or use the measurement saver below to record your results.

02

Measure the Length

Measure one side of the floor from one wall to the opposite wall. Record the result in metres.

03

Measure the Width

Measure the other side of the floor, from one wall to the opposite wall. Record this number too.

04

Calculate the Area

Multiply the length by the width to find the floor area in square metres (m²).

The simple formula

How Do You Calculate Floor Area?

Area = Length × Width

For example, a room that is 5 metres long and 4 metres wide has an area of 20 square metres.

5 m × 4 m = 20 m²

This calculation assumes your room has a rectangular floor. For an irregularly shaped room, divide the floor into smaller rectangles, calculate each area, and add them together.

Before buying tiles

Remember to Allow for Extra Tiles

Your room's floor area is a starting point, not always the exact amount of tiles to purchase. Cutting, breakage, the tile layout, and the coverage stated on each carton can affect the quantity you need.
