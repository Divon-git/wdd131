Nigeria is a country in West Africa that gained independence from British colonial rule on October 1, 1960. It is Africa’s most populous country and is home to over 250 ethnic groups, including the Hausa, Yoruba, and Igbo. Nigeria is known for its rich culture, diverse traditions, music, film industry, and natural resources. Its capital is Abuja, while Lagos is its largest city and a major economic center.
